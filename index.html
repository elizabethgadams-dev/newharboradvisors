About

I've been where
you are now.

When my husband died in 2024, I had to figure out my financial future on my own — with two kids, properties across two states, and no one I could call who really understood the situation.

I wanted someone I could pay by the hour for honest help. Not someone who needed to manage my assets to make it worth their time. I couldn't find that person.

So I decided to become her. I'm currently completing my financial planning credentials and building New Harbor Advisors for women in the same position I was in — recently widowed or divorced, suddenly responsible for a financial picture they didn't set up alone, and not sure what to do next.

My background is in technology consulting. I've also been investing in rental real estate since 2014 and have been working toward financial independence for most of my adult life. I'm not a traditional financial advisor — and that's the point.

My Philosophy

"Most people are more capable of managing their own money than the financial industry gives them credit for."

What changes during a major life transition isn't your capability. It's your bandwidth, your confidence, and your access to the right information at the right moment.

My job isn't to take over. It's to help you get oriented, make a plan, and feel like you know what you're doing again.
