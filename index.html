About

I've been where
you are now.

My husband died in March 2024. I had a four-year-old and an eight-month-old, properties in two states, a life insurance payout on the way, and a financial future that I suddenly had to figure out.

I wanted someone I could pay by the hour for honest help. Someone who understood this specific situation (not just the numbers, but the weight of making big decisions while you're still in shock). I couldn't find her.

So I decided to become her. I've been investing in real estate since 2014. I've spent most of my adult life working toward financial independence. None of that is typical for this space. That's the point.

I also work with women navigating divorce, who face many of the same decisions with a different kind of pressure.

My Philosophy

"Most people are more capable of managing their own money than the financial industry gives them credit for."

What changes during a major life transition isn't your capability. It's your bandwidth, your confidence, and your access to the right information at the right moment.

My job isn't to take over. It's to help you get oriented, make a plan, and feel like you know what you're doing again.
